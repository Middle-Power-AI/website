Research

Our research focuses on three areas:

Sovereign AI Capabilities

Exploring alternative AI architectures, technological breakthroughs, and multinational cooperation that could open new pathways to sovereign frontier AI development.

The Politics of AI

Examining how AI is transforming relationships between states, private enterprises, and citizens, and the implications for the future distribution of political and economic power.

Sovereignty Under AI Asymmetry

Investigating how middle powers can preserve their independence in a world where overwhelming AI dominance could become a tool of geopolitical coercion.
