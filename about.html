About

Middle Power AI was founded by Jason Yung, a former Canadian diplomat and creative technologist whose work spans international security, emerging technologies, and AI governance.

Jason previously served in senior policy roles at Global Affairs Canada and the Department of National Defence, working on international security cooperation and defence AI governance. He holds a master's degree from NYU's Interactive Telecommunications Program.

Middle Power AI brings together perspectives from geopolitics, technology, and public policy to investigate how states can navigate the emerging AI order.
